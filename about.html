About

I'm a software engineer from Scotland. My professional interests include:

  • Cloud-native tooling, Kubernetes, and distributed systems,
  • Identity, security, authn, authz, and emerging standards in these areas,
  • Building useful data-driven tools, products, and services.

At present, I'm an engineer at Cisco Systems in the Hyperfabric team.

Earlier in my career, I worked in engineering research at the University of Strathclyde: developing statistical and machine learning methods for reliability in nuclear generation condition monitoring. After an extended write-up period, I successfully defended my thesis in January 2019 and received my doctorate in November of the same year.

I live in the south of Scotland with my family and our Scottish Terrier.
